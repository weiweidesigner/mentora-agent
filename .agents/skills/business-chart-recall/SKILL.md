For major US ETFs today / intraday price trends, market_trackers.py --category etfs reads the official ETF list and real minute prices, with selectable quote cards and periods. Single-instrument technical analysis uses K-lines. K-lines support equities, ETFs, crypto assets, and resolvable option contracts; company fundamentals and other business modules remain limited to applicable US-listed securities.
