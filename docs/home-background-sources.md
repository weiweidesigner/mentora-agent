# Homepage background videos

- Dark mode: existing local market animation, unchanged.
- Light mode: user-selected “[4K] Blue Gradient Screensaver Video | Perfect for mood lighting, background, TV art” by Calming Visuals – 100% Human Made Abstract Visuals.
  Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UExTwU4iwEE
  Supplied by the site owner on 2026-10-15.
  Local background asset: `app/static/assets/home-light-background.mp4`.
  Derived from the opening 62 seconds: 720p, 24 fps, muted; a two-second dissolve joins the loop. Poster is derived from the loop's first frame.

Theme changes switch both video and poster. The video scrolls with the homepage hero, fades to the page background, and pauses when offscreen, when Tasks is open, when the document is hidden, or when reduced motion / data saving is enabled.
